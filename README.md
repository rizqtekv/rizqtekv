# Lumidoku

### Calm Sudoku for Android

Lumidoku is a Sudoku game designed around a calm, focused puzzle experience — playable anywhere, with or without an internet connection.

It combines familiar Sudoku with alternative puzzle formats, daily challenges and a technique-learning experience.

---

## 🎯 What is Lumidoku?

Lumidoku is built for players who want more than a basic Sudoku board.

The app currently includes:

- Classic 9×9 Sudoku
- Mini 6×6 Sudoku
- X-Sudoku
- Jigsaw Sudoku
- Killer Sudoku
- Daily challenges
- Weekly challenges
- Streaks and streak freezes
- Monthly trophies
- Technique Academy
- English and Hindi
- Light and dark themes
- Large-text support
- Android TalkBack support

---

## 🧩 Sudoku Modes

### Classic

The traditional 9×9 Sudoku experience.

### Mini

A smaller 6×6 Sudoku board designed for shorter puzzle sessions.

### X-Sudoku

A Sudoku variant that adds diagonal constraints.

### Jigsaw

A Sudoku variant using irregular regions.

### Killer

A Sudoku variant that introduces cage-based constraints.

---

## 🎓 Technique Academy

Lumidoku includes a Technique Academy designed to help players understand Sudoku solving techniques through real puzzle positions.

The current experience includes 14 lessons.

The goal is not simply to give players answers, but to help them understand how Sudoku can be solved logically.

---

## 📅 Challenges & Progress

Lumidoku includes a progression system designed around regular puzzle sessions.

Current features include:

- Daily challenges
- Weekly challenges
- Streaks
- Streak freezes
- Monthly trophies

These features are designed to encourage consistent play without turning Sudoku into a high-pressure experience.

## ♿ Accessibility

Accessibility is an important part of Lumidoku.

The app currently supports:

- Large text
- Light theme
- Dark theme
- English
- Hindi
- Android TalkBack

Accessibility behavior can vary depending on the Android version, device manufacturer and system accessibility settings.

If you encounter an accessibility problem, please contact support.

## 🔒 Privacy

Lumidoku does not use its own account system or backend service for player game data.

Game progress, statistics, streaks, settings and reminder choices are stored on the device according to the current privacy policy.

Lumidoku may use Google AdMob for advertising and Google Play for purchases.
