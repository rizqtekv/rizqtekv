01

About Lumidoku

What is Lumidoku?

Lumidoku is a Sudoku game for Android focused on a calm, accessible and focused puzzle experience.

Who is Lumidoku for?

Lumidoku is designed for people who enjoy Sudoku, from players who want a familiar Classic board to players looking for more challenging Sudoku variants.

Is Lumidoku only for experienced Sudoku players?

No. You can start with Classic or Mini Sudoku and use the Technique Academy to gradually learn more solving techniques.

02

Gameplay

Which Sudoku modes are available?

Lumidoku includes five puzzle styles:

  • Classic 9×9
  • Mini 6×6
  • X-Sudoku
  • Jigsaw
  • Killer
What is Classic Sudoku?

Classic Sudoku uses the familiar 9×9 grid where the objective is to complete the board while following standard Sudoku rules.

What is Mini Sudoku?

Mini Sudoku uses a smaller 6×6 board for a more compact puzzle experience.

What is X-Sudoku?

X-Sudoku adds diagonal constraints to the standard Sudoku challenge.

What is Jigsaw Sudoku?

Jigsaw Sudoku uses irregular regions instead of the standard 3×3 regions.

What is Killer Sudoku?

Killer Sudoku adds cage-based constraints to the puzzle, creating a different type of logical challenge.

Can I play Lumidoku without internet?

Lumidoku is designed to be played without requiring an internet connection for its core Sudoku experience.

03

Challenges & Progress

Does Lumidoku have daily challenges?

Yes. Lumidoku includes daily challenges designed to give players a reason to return for a focused puzzle session.

Are there weekly challenges?

Yes. Lumidoku includes weekly challenges in addition to its daily challenge experience.

What are streak freezes?

Streak freezes are part of Lumidoku's challenge and progression experience. Their purpose is to help protect a challenge streak when a player misses a day.

What are monthly trophies?

Monthly trophies are part of Lumidoku's progression system and provide an additional way to recognize continued challenge participation.

04

Technique Academy

What is the Technique Academy?

The Technique Academy is a learning area that teaches Sudoku solving techniques using real puzzle positions.

How many lessons are available?

Lumidoku currently describes the Technique Academy as containing 14 lessons.

Do I need to complete the Academy before playing Sudoku?

No. The Academy is a learning feature. You can play Sudoku independently and use the Academy when you want to improve your solving knowledge.

Privacy & Data

Where is my game progress stored?

According to the Lumidoku Privacy Policy, games, statistics, streaks, settings and reminder choices are stored on your device.

Does Lumidoku have an account system?

No. Lumidoku does not use an account system according to its current Privacy Policy.

Does Lumidoku show advertisements?

Yes. The current Privacy Policy states that Lumidoku uses Google AdMob for advertising.

Can I remove advertisements?

The current Privacy Policy states that users can permanently remove banner and full-screen advertisements through the Remove Ads purchase.

Does Lumidoku use Google Play for purchases?

Yes. Purchases are processed through Google Play.

What information should I include in a bug report?
