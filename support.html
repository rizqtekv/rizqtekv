Reporting a technical problem

If something isn't working correctly, a few extra details can make it much easier for us to investigate.

  • Your Android version
  • Your Lumidoku app version, if available
  • The Sudoku mode you were playing
  • What you expected to happen
  • What actually happened
  • The steps that caused the problem
  • A screenshot or screen recording, if useful

Please do not send passwords, payment information, authentication codes or other sensitive information in a support email.

Quick answers

Some common support questions can be answered without sending an email.

Can Lumidoku be played without internet?

Lumidoku is designed to be played without requiring an internet connection for its core Sudoku experience.

Which Sudoku modes are available?

Lumidoku includes Classic 9×9, Mini 6×6, X-Sudoku, Jigsaw and Killer.

Does Lumidoku support Hindi?

Yes. Lumidoku supports English and Hindi.
