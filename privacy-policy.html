Privacy

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 October 2026

Lumidoku is an offline Sudoku game. This page explains what the app does with information. It is written in plain language; if something is unclear, write to support@rizqtek.com .

What stays on your phone

Your games, statistics, streaks, settings and reminder choice are stored only on your device.

Lumidoku has no account system and no server of its own, so we never receive this information.

Uninstalling the app removes it.

If you switch on the daily reminder, the notification is created on your phone at the time you chose. No information is sent anywhere to do this.

Backup

If Android Auto Backup is switched on for your phone, your saved games and settings may be included in your Google account's device backup, so they can come back when you set up a new phone.

That backup is managed by Google, not by us, and you can turn it off in your phone's settings.

Advertising

Lumidoku shows ads from Google AdMob.

To show and measure ads, the AdMob software on your phone may collect and send to Google:

  • Your device's advertising ID
  • Your approximate location, derived from your IP address
  • How you interact with ads
  • Diagnostic information such as crashes and performance

We do not receive this data about you personally.

Google's use of this information is described in the Google advertising policies and Google privacy policy .

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or another region that requires it, Lumidoku asks for your choice before any ad is requested.

You can change it any time in Settings → Privacy options .

On Android you can also reset your advertising ID or opt out of ads personalisation in your phone's Settings → Privacy → Ads .

You can remove banner and full-screen ads permanently with the Remove Ads purchase. Watching a reward video is always optional.

Purchases

Purchases are processed by Google Play.

We do not receive your payment details; we only learn, through Google Play, that the Remove Ads purchase is active so that ads can be switched off.

Children

Lumidoku is meant for people aged 13 and over.

It is not directed at children under 13, and ads are limited to teen-appropriate content.

Sharing a puzzle

When you choose to share a result, the picture and challenge code are handed to the app you pick (for example, a messenger).

That is your choice each time; Lumidoku does not share anything on its own.

A challenge code only contains the board type, difficulty, puzzle number and your time; it contains no personal information.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address with a new date.
