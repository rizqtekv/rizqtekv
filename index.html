Frequently asked questions.

Quick answers about Lumidoku.

What is Lumidoku?

Lumidoku is a Sudoku game for Android focused on a calm and accessible puzzle experience.

Which Sudoku modes are available?

Lumidoku includes Classic 9×9, Mini 6×6, X-Sudoku, Jigsaw and Killer boards.

Can I play without internet?

Lumidoku is designed to be played without requiring an internet connection for its core Sudoku experience.

Does Lumidoku support Hindi?

Yes. Lumidoku supports English and Hindi.

Does Lumidoku support accessibility?

Lumidoku includes large text, light and dark themes and TalkBack support.

How can I contact support?

Email support@rizqtek.com with your question or feedback.

Have an idea or found a problem?

We welcome feedback, bug reports and ideas that can make Lumidoku a better Sudoku experience.
